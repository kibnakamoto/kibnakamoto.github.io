I learn by building with my hands.

Outside code and test benches, I've worked in an auto shop, built furniture, and spent time on construction and roofing crews. It's why I'm comfortable at a dynamometer as well as a keyboard.

Auto shop

I diagnosed and repaired mechanical problems on vehicles, and took engine components apart and put them back together with the proper tools and procedures, working with minimal supervision and following shop safety rules.

Woodworking

I designed and built a coffee table and carved a wooden cup, using saws, drills, sanding equipment and workshop machinery. Measuring, cutting and assembling to tolerance is the same discipline as getting a wiring harness right.

Electronics and robotics

I built a remote-controlled Arduino car from the wiring up, with an H-bridge motor driver, DC motors and logic gates, and wrote the Bluetooth control app for Android. I also tutored students in Arduino motor control.
